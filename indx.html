Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

NEXUS Futuristic Time System ("NEXUS", "we", "our", or "the extension") is designed with user privacy in mind.

Information We Access

NEXUS may request access to Google Calendar information when the user voluntarily chooses to connect their Google Calendar.

Calendar access is used only to provide calendar-related functionality within the extension.

Google Account Information

When Google Calendar integration is enabled, authentication is handled through Google's OAuth authorization system. NEXUS does not ask users to provide their Google password.

Data Storage

NEXUS does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information. The extension only uses information necessary to provide its features.

Third-Party Services

Google services may be used for calendar authentication and calendar data access. Google's own privacy policies and terms apply to those services.
